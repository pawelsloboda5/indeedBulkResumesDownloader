# Send me the log

Something went wrong and I asked for the log. Four steps.

**1.** Open the folder holding `IndeedCVDownloader.exe`.

**2.** Go into `logs\`.

**3.** Attach **one file**: `latest.log`

If `latest.log` is missing, or its date is not today's run, attach the
newest `run_....log` instead — that name has the date and time in it, so
sort by Date modified and take the top one. Closing the console window
mid-run stops `latest.log` from being updated.

**4.** Email or Teams it to me. Attach the file itself; don't paste it into
the message.

---

**Send only that one file.** Don't zip or send the whole `logs\` folder — it
also holds your live Indeed sign-in, which nobody should receive.

**It contains applicant names**, so send it the same way you'd send any
candidate information: internal email or Teams to me directly, not a public
link or an outside address.

If I ask for anything else — a screenshot of the browser's Network tab, a
"Copy as cURL" — check with me first. That output usually carries your
sign-in token.
